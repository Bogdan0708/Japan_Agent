# Mandate guidance: frontier ("revolutionary technology") sleeve

The mandate covers Japanese new-technology themes. Frontier exposure is a
distinct sleeve, not a loosening of the core mandate. The risk engine enforces
`FRONTIER` with the same £15 absolute cap as `SATELLITE`; conviction never buys
a bigger limit.

## Reference structure for the £100 learning portfolio

- ~£40 diversified Japan core (respects the 40% NAV cap)
- ~£40 automation/technology ETF
- ≤£15 frontier satellite
- ≥£5 cash floor (engine-enforced)

This is a mandate structure the agent proposes against, not a standing
instruction to buy those allocations.

## Frontier theme universe (research leads, not theses)

Japan's Integrated Innovation Strategy 2026 names AI and advanced robotics,
semiconductors and communications, quantum, fusion, space, advanced materials,
mobility/logistics, biotechnology, and energy-security/GX technologies as
strategic areas. METI's semiconductor strategy emphasises the full AI
ecosystem — models, compute, communications, electricity, security, physical
AI — not merely chip manufacturers. Government support is a reason to research
a company, never by itself a reason to buy it.

## Requirements for any frontier candidate

Every frontier proposal must document, with citations that pass the
deterministic evidence check:

1. Commercial adoption, revenue/backlog, or a credible route to it.
2. A defensible supply-chain position, IP, or switching cost.
3. Balance-sheet/runway analysis for capital-intensive technologies.
4. Valuation and dilution analysis.
5. Named catalysts and a falsifiable invalidation condition.
6. Exact T212 availability, fractional support, KID status, liquidity, and
   overlap with existing ETF holdings.
7. At least two primary sources, including a filing or official disclosure.

## Access reality

Most genuinely early-stage Japanese companies are not reachable through T212's
LSE/NYSE universe. Frontier exposure will often be indirect — established
public parents or UCITS thematic funds — with lower thematic purity. Say so in
the thesis rather than overstating the exposure.
